App: Loop Leg
Developer: Arthur Ramsey
Contact: arthur.ramsey11@outlook.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Loop Leg is an offline iOS tool for an expansion loop on a pipe rack. You drag the outer leg, and the original length and the temperature rise decide which built leg falls short. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Line names, lengths, outside diameters, temperature rises, coefficients, stresses, and built leg lengths stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Motion. Motion is used only when you tap Read the lean. The angle stays on the device and is not used in the leg length.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet or export a CSV summary, the content you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
