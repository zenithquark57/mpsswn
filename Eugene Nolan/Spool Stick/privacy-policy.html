Privacy Policy

App: Spool Stick
Developer: Eugene Nolan
Contact: eugene.nolan33@163.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Spool Stick is an offline iOS tool for a pipe spool. You drag cuts onto a centerline, subtract each weld gap, and see which piece leaves the end fitting off the far mark. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Spool names, cut lengths, weld gaps, centerline marks, and an optional spoken heat number stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Microphone. The microphone is used only when you tap Record heat number. The recording stays on the device and is not used in the closure.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet or export a CSV summary, the content you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
