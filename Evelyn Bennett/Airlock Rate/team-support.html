Airlock Rate support

Developer: Evelyn Bennett
Contact: evelyn.bennett85@163.com

Airlock Rate keeps one cellar of fermentations on this iPhone. Tap each bubble you hear, or let a short listen count them. The rate is bubbles per minute.

Log a gravity reading. The number is corrected for the sample temperature. Two readings about 48 hours apart, together with a quiet airlock, mark the batch ready to bottle. Priming sugar is the grams to add for the volumes you want.

Share writes a CSV through the system share sheet. There is no account and nothing to buy.
