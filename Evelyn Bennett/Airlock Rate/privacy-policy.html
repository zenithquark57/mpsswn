App: Airlock Rate

Effective: 2026-11-17

Airlock Rate is an offline fermentation log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Batch names, gravity readings, bubble counts, bottle marks, and reminder times stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that cellar data.

Microphone. While you listen, the microphone counts bubbles at the airlock. The recording is not kept. Tap count works without the microphone.

Notifications. A reminder is scheduled on this iPhone only when you ask for the next reading.

Sharing. If you share a CSV of the batch, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
