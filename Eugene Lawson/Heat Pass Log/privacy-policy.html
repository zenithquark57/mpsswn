Privacy Policy

App: Heat Pass Log
Developer: Eugene Lawson
Contact: eugene.lawson41@gmail.com
Effective: November 18, 2026

Heat Pass Log is an offline iOS tool for logging weld-pass voltage, current, and travel speed, computing heat input, and comparing it to your WPS window. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Welds, pass readings, WPS limits, and optional nameplate photos you capture stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload your measurements to our servers.

Camera. If you allow camera access, photos are stored only on the device as local references.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet or export a file, the content you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
