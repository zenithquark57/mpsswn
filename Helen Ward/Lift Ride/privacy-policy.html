Privacy Policy

App: Lift Ride
Developer: Helen Ward
Contact: helen.ward23@gmail.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Lift Ride is an offline elevator ride-quality log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Lift names, floor heights, and ride readings stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that ride data.

Motion. When you record a ride, motion samples are used on this iPhone to score the trip. The samples are not uploaded.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
