Privacy Policy

App: Cleanroom Class
Developer: Albert Mercer
Contact: albert.mercer20@163.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Cleanroom Class is an offline room-grading log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Room names, sizes, target classes, particle counts, and verdicts stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that room data.

Files. If you import a counter CSV, the file is read on this iPhone and the counts are kept here.

Sharing. If you share a report, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
