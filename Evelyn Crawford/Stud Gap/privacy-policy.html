Effective: November 19, 2026

Stud Gap is an offline iOS tool for a flange face. You drag one stud off the bolt circle, subtract the hole from the chord, and see which stud lets the ligament or the edge fall short. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Flange names, stud angles, radii, hole size, and the ligament and edge limits stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Location. Location is used only when you tap Stamp this place. The coordinate stays on the device and is not used in the ligament.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet or export a CSV summary, the content you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
