App: Grade Run

Effective: November 19, 2026

Grade Run is an offline iOS tool for a drain profile. You drag one well invert, and the drop over the spacing names the reach that is flatter than the minimum you wrote. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Well names, invert levels, spacings, and the minimum grade stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Location. Location is used only when you tap Stamp this well. The coordinate stays on the device and is not used in the grade.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the well list you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
