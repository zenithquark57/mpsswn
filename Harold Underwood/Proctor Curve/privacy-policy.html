Privacy Policy

App: Proctor Curve
Developer: Harold Underwood
Contact: harold.underwood95@163.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Proctor Curve is an offline soil compaction (Proctor) curve tool for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Soil records, moisture–density points, field readings, and optional short hammer-blow audio used to count blows stay on this iPhone. Audio is processed on device and is not uploaded by the app.

Microphone. If you use blow counting, the app records a short clip on this iPhone only to count peaks. You can refuse microphone access and still use the rest of the app.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.
