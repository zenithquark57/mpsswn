Privacy Policy

App: Charge Band
Developer: Evelyn Harmon
Contact: evelyn.harmon61@outlook.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Charge Band is an offline iOS tool for naming a refrigerant-circuit fault from suction and liquid readings on one visit. Superheat and subcooling are read together against that circuit’s own target band. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Circuit names, target bands, gauge readings, visits, and an optional nameplate photo stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those readings to our servers.

Photos. The photo picker stores the picture you choose only on the device, as a nameplate reference. It is not used in the calculation.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet or export a CSV summary, the content you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
