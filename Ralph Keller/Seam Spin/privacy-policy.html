Privacy Policy

App: Seam Spin
Developer: Ralph Keller
Contact: ralph.keller53@163.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Seam Spin keeps each quilt block's seams on this iPhone. A crossing lies flat when its four seams already chase one way. If it does not, the sentence names one seam and one direction. Spin presses only that seam.

The app does not require an account and has no in-app purchases. Records stay on this iPhone.
