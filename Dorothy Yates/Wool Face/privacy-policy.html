App: Wool Face

Effective: November 19, 2026

Wool Face is an offline iOS tool for a cold-pipe jacket. You drag the outer wool, and the face temperature names the layer that falls below the dew point. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Layer names, thicknesses, and the dew point stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Microphone. The microphone is used only when you tap Note the face. The sound stays on the device and is not used in the temperature.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the layer list you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
