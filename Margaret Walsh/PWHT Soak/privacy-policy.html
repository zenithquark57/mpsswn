Effective: November 19, 2026

PWHT Soak is an offline iOS tool for recording thermocouple time and temperature on a weld joint, marking who entered the soak band last and who left first, and checking continuous hold time plus heat and cool rates against the procedure you wrote. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Joints, procedure limits, thermocouple readings, and optional chart photos you choose stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload your measurements to our servers.

Photos. If you allow photo library access, the picture you pick is stored only on the device as a local chart reference. It is not used in the calculation.

Files. If you import a recorder CSV, that file is read on the device. The app does not send the file to a server.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet or export a file, the content you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
