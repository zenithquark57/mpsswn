Privacy Policy

App: Carb Tach
Developer: Helen Zimmerman
Contact: helen.zimmerman60@yahoo.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Carb Tach is an offline two-stroke carburetor log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Machine names, needle turns, and engine-speed readings stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that machine data.

Microphone. When you listen for an engine, the sample is used on this iPhone to estimate speed. The sample is not uploaded.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
