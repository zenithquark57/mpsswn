App: Side Stake
Developer: Irene Ramsey
Contact: irene.ramsey13@gmail.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Side Stake keeps each round of a basket on this iPhone. The circumference and the widest gap decide which spokes take a second stake. One spoke holds at most two. When that count would be even, one short bye-stake makes it odd so the plain weave can keep going.

The app does not require an account and has no in-app purchases. Records stay on this iPhone.
