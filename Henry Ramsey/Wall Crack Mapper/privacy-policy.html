Privacy Policy

App: Wall Crack Mapper
Developer: Henry Ramsey
Contact: henry.ramsey80@sina.com
Effective: 2026-11-17

Wall Crack Mapper is an offline masonry crack log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Site names, crack widths, and gauge readings stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that survey data.

Camera and heading. When you photograph a facade or take a heading, the photo and heading stay on this iPhone. They are not uploaded.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
