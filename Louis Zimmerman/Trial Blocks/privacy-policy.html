Privacy Policy

App: Trial Blocks
Developer: Louis Zimmerman

Effective: 2026-11-17

Trial Blocks is an offline field-trial log for iPhone. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on this iPhone. Trial names, plot layouts, yields, and estimates stay on this iPhone. The app does not upload that trial data.

Location. On the walk page, location is used only to show which plot you are standing on. The demo does not request location. Coordinates are not uploaded.

Sharing. If you share a CSV, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.
