Trial Blocks keeps randomized complete block trials on this iPhone. Each block is shuffled on its own. Enter a yield for each plot.

Mark a plot missing and the Yates estimate fills that cell for the analysis only. Means show compact letter groups from the least significant difference.

There is no account and nothing to buy.
