Privacy Policy

App: Pipe Ovality
Developer: Ralph Walsh
Contact: ralph.walsh48@outlook.com
Effective: November 18, 2026

Pipe Ovality is an offline iOS tool for recording clock-position outside diameters on pipe ends or girth welds and computing ovality. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Sections, OD readings, and optional nameplate photos you capture stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload your measurements to our servers.

Camera. If you allow camera access, photos are stored only on the device as local references.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the content you choose to share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
